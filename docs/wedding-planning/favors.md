## ☕ "The Perfect Blend" coffee favors — _idea captured 2026-06-24, decision pending_

**Concept:** Personalized coffee favor pouches printed with **"The Perfect Blend"** + our names + wedding date. Pairs intentionally with the **coffee-cart** vendor decision (Monarch Espresso / Kuppa Joy / Sunflowers & Grace) for a cohesive coffee theme.

**What it is:** Not a single shop — a popular favor concept sold by many Etsy sellers (also Amazon/Zazzle/specialty roasters). Standard form: a **~4"×6" resealable kraft pouch (~2 oz capacity)**, personalized. Top Etsy sellers are well-reviewed (5-star "Star Sellers," ~2–3 day production).

**The cost-driving decision — fill them ourselves vs. buy pre-filled:**

| Option | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bags only** (we add coffee) | **~$0.75–$0.90 / bag** personalized | e.g. 40-pack ≈ $34.80 (~$0.87 ea). Cheapest; we source + fill + seal. |
| **Pre-filled w/ coffee** | **~$4.50 / favor** | e.g. Dean's Beans 4 oz; Etsy coffee-included sellers similar, often 2-set minimums. Turnkey, no DIY. |

**Rough total @ ~100 favors:**
- **DIY-filled:** ~$85 bags + ~$125–150 bulk coffee (≈8 bags/lb @ $10–12/lb) → **~$210–240 all-in (~$2.20 ea)** + assembly labor.
- **Pre-filled:** **~$450 (~$4.50 ea)** + zero assembly.

**Watch-outs:**
1. Most Etsy "Perfect Blend" listings are **bags only — coffee NOT included** (easy to miss).
2. DIY route hides a **labor cost** (filling/sealing 100+ pouches).

**Synergy angle:** source the fill-coffee in **bulk from whichever coffee-cart vendor we book** — ties the favor to the cart and may beat retail bean pricing.

**Next steps (when we act):**
- Decide bags-only vs pre-filled (drives budget + labor).
- If bags-only: pick a roaster / ask the cart vendor for bulk beans.
- Pick the single best-reviewed Etsy listing that matches our colors; price for actual final guest count.
