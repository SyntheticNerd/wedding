## 🍸 Bar / Alcohol — three options (alcohol is the budget squeeze)
1. **No Host Bar** — guests buy their own; you pick 6 bottled/canned drinks + house wines + Pepsi.
   **$100 setup fee, no minimum.** Optional *pre-paid* variant (you fund a tab until it runs out).
2. **Purchase from Wolf Lakes** — you pay for all the alcohol for the night from their list.
   Wine **by the case** (12 btls): Salmon Creek $160 → Justin $410. Champagne $160–$320/case.
   Beer **kegs** (~140 cups) $245–$285; bottled/canned $70/24.
3. **Bring In / Pay Fees** — bring your **own wine & champagne**; pay **$12/bottle corkage**
   (includes bartender, glassware, service). ⚠️ **Beer must be bought through Wolf Lakes** (can't BYO beer).
   *Prices exclude sales tax. Hand-pouring included in champagne prices.*

> Likely cheapest for a budget: **Option 3 (BYO wine/champagne at $12/btl corkage)** + a small beer order — but model it once guest drink counts are estimated.

## 🍽️ Plated Wedding Menu (all-inclusive, included in the $16,094 venue price)
Dinner includes rolls & butter, water, beverage station (lemonade punch, iced tea, coffee). Pick:
- **Appetizers — choose 4** (e.g. BBQ/Swedish meatballs, egg/spring rolls, sausage, wings, shrimp cocktail, stuffed mushrooms, spinach-artichoke dip, cheese & crackers, fruit/veggie trays, caravan sandwiches).
- **Entrées — choose 2:** Beef (NY steak, beef tornadoes, tri-tip) · Chicken (apricot bourbon, cordon bleu, lemon pepper, stuffed, parmesan) · Seafood (coconut shrimp, skewered shrimp, mesquite salmon) · Pasta (shrimp scampi, spinach-cheese lasagna, veggie alfredo penne).
- **Salad — choose 1** (garden, caesar, spring mix) · **Hot veg — choose 1** (summer medley, green beans w/ bacon, winter medley) · **Rice/Potato — choose 1** (rice pilaf, garlic mashed, red potato, golden potatoes).

## 🥂 Rehearsal Dinner — "The Feedlot at Wolf Lakes" (optional add-on)
- **$48.50/person** + **18% service** + sales tax. **25 guest min / 80 max.** 2-hour limit.
  Held in The Feedlot or The Corral.
- Food: choose **2 appetizers + 2 entrées** (chef picks salad/starch/veg/dessert). **Same entrée for all adults.**
- Kids (≤8): chicken strips or corn dog + fries, **$10**. Kids don't count toward the minimum.
- **$500 non-refundable deposit** + signed contract to book. Final headcount due **3 weeks prior** (can increase, not decrease). Balance due **1 week prior**.
- Alcohol: same purchase-from-venue model (wine by the case unless Salmon Creek, which is per-bottle).
