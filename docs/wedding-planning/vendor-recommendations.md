## 🌸 Florist
**TOP — Kiku Floral (559-266-5480).** Deepest track record: WeddingWire 4.9 (~99 reviews),
multiple Couples' Choice Awards. Reviews call out candle/tea-light centerpieces — fits the
candlelit look. ~$3k starting (more accessible than Sweet Memories). Family-owned ~40 yrs.
- #2 — **Sweet Memories (559-625-5242)** — closest to romantic/garden aesthetic but pricier
  ($5k+); confirm they do full drapery/candle/lighting installs, not just florals. Yelp 4.5 (~18).
- **Drapery note — Sweet Dreams Floral (559-641-7606)** is the only one that explicitly
  advertises **pipe-and-drape** (Yelp 4.8/137). Quote here too if drapery is a hard requirement.

## 🎂 Bakery
**TOP — Frosted Cakery (559-917-8880).** Refined, design-forward buttercream; largest review
volume (Yelp ~376; WeddingWire 4.9). Watch a recurring "communication during ordering" theme —
lock design details in writing.
- #2 — **Barb's Cakery (559-432-2272)** — best value, closest to venue (~10 mi). Great taste
  rep (WeddingWire 4.9/~37). Keep the design classic; confirm date reservation in writing.
- Sweet Dreams Cakery — ~1 hr away (Oakhurst), thin cake-specific reviews. Call, don't lead with it.

## 📷 Photo / Video
**TOP — Hooper Photo & Film (559-513-7210).** Best single-vendor photo+video (husband-wife,
both in-house). ~$5,500 photo / ~$8,500 photo+video. Style is candid/authentic — review the
portfolio to confirm it reads romantic enough.
- #2 — **Franklin and Brianne (559-240-5570)** — warmest, most romantic photography (Birdeye
  5.0/48). Some video capability — confirm full films vs ceremony-only.
- **Highest ceiling (two teams):** Franklin & Brianne (photo) + **Ironfire Studio** (cinematic
  films, video-only) — best aesthetic result if you'll coordinate two vendors.
- **Avoid / verify:** Reverie (Clovis storefront listed CLOSED May 2026 — confirm status);
  Mark Janzen (documentary, not romantic); Lomeli (generalist, one bad wedding-day review).
